About Dr. Mitchell

Dr. Sarah Mitchell founded BrightSmile Dental in 2012 after nearly a decade working in group practices across the Pacific Northwest. Her vision was simple: build a practice where patients are treated like family — not a number — and where every clinical decision is made with long-term oral health in mind, not short-term production goals.

Growing up in a household where dentist visits meant judgment and discomfort, Dr. Mitchell decided early in her career to make patient comfort a clinical priority, not an afterthought. She completed advanced training in sedation dentistry and anxiety-reduction techniques, and today nearly a third of her patients are individuals who hadn't seen a dentist in years because of fear.

"The biggest compliment I get is when someone tells me they actually don't mind coming to the dentist anymore," she says. "That's the whole point."

Areas of Expertise

General Dentistry

Comprehensive exams, cleanings, fillings, extractions, root canals, and preventive care for patients of all ages.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Porcelain veneers, composite bonding, teeth whitening, and full smile makeovers tailored to each patient's face and goals.

Dental Implants

Single-tooth implants, implant-supported bridges, and implant consultations for patients with missing or failing teeth.

Invisalign

Clear aligner treatment for mild to moderate alignment concerns, including retainer follow-up care.

Sedation Dentistry

Oral conscious sedation and nitrous oxide options for anxious patients or lengthy procedures requiring deep relaxation.

Restorative Care

Crowns, bridges, inlays, onlays, and full-arch reconstruction for patients with complex restorative needs.

Beyond the Office

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Mitchell is a dedicated mother of two and an avid trail runner. She serves on the board of the local community health clinic, where she provides pro-bono dental screenings twice a year. She also mentors dental students through the University of Oregon's alumni network and speaks at regional dental conferences on topics related to patient anxiety and practice culture.
