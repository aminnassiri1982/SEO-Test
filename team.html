Meet Our Team

Behind every healthy smile is a team that genuinely cares. Get to know the people who make your experience at BrightSmile Dental warm, professional, and stress-free.

Lead Dentist & Practice Owner

Dr. Sarah Mitchell, DDS

Dr. Sarah Mitchell founded BrightSmile Dental in 2012 with one mission: to make exceptional dental care accessible and anxiety-free for every patient. A graduate of the University of Michigan School of Dentistry, she brings over 18 years of clinical experience covering general dentistry, cosmetic procedures, and dental implants. Dr. Mitchell is known for her gentle chairside manner and her commitment to staying current with the latest advances in dental technology.

DDS, University of Michigan Member, American Dental Association Invisalign Certified Provider

The Full Team

Registered Dental Hygienist

Lisa Torres, RDH

Lisa has been with BrightSmile Dental for over a decade and is the friendly face most patients see at their cleaning appointments. With a degree in Dental Hygiene from Portland Community College and a talent for patient education, Lisa has a gift for making even the most anxious patients feel at ease. Outside the office, she volunteers with community free-clinic events and coaches youth soccer on weekends.

Dental Hygienist

Kevin Park, RDH

Kevin joined our practice in 2019 after several years working in periodontal specialty clinics, which gives him a sharp eye for early-stage gum disease and a thorough approach to preventive care. Patients consistently praise his detailed explanations and the way he takes time to answer every question without making anyone feel rushed. When he's not cleaning teeth, Kevin is an avid cyclist and amateur photographer.

Dental Assistant

Amanda Brooks, CDA

Amanda is a Certified Dental Assistant with eight years of hands-on experience assisting in everything from routine fillings to complex restorative procedures. Her calm energy and quick efficiency keep our operatories running smoothly, and her warm personality helps patients relax the moment they sit in the chair. Amanda is also certified in dental radiography and coronal polishing, and she stays current through annual CE coursework.

Dental Assistant

Jordan Ellis

Jordan is our newest dental assistant and has quickly become a beloved member of the team. After completing the Dental Assisting program at City College with honors, Jordan brought an infectious enthusiasm and sharp attention to detail that our patients and providers notice right away. Jordan is especially skilled at digital radiography and enjoys helping patients understand their X-rays in plain language.

Practice Manager

Rachel Nguyen

Rachel is the organizational backbone of BrightSmile Dental. With a background in healthcare administration and 12 years of experience managing dental practices, she keeps every aspect of the office running at its best — from staff scheduling and compliance to patient experience initiatives. Rachel's dedication to continuous improvement has helped BrightSmile earn top patient-satisfaction ratings year after year.

Patient Coordinator

Tom Vasquez

Tom is usually the first voice you hear when you call BrightSmile Dental and the first smile you see at our front desk. He handles appointment scheduling, new patient onboarding, and appointment reminders with patience and genuine warmth. Tom speaks fluent Spanish, which helps our Spanish-speaking patients feel fully informed and comfortable throughout their visit. He's also our go-to person for any questions about treatment timelines.

Billing & Insurance Specialist

Nina Okafor

Nina takes the confusion out of dental billing. With over nine years of experience in dental insurance verification and claims processing, she works tirelessly to maximize your benefits and minimize out-of-pocket costs. Nina is well-versed in most major insurance plans and is always willing to sit down with patients to walk through their coverage, co-pays, and payment plan options — no jargon, no stress.
