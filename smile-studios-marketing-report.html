⚠ Blog Strategy & Keyword Cannibalization Risk

Keyword cannibalization occurs when multiple pages on your site compete for the same search term. This confuses Google about which page to rank, often resulting in neither page ranking well. As Smile Studios builds out blog content, it is critical to avoid writing blog posts that target the same primary keywords as existing (or planned) service pages.

Key Rule: Service pages should target transactional keywords (e.g., "emergency dentist Bakersfield"). Blog posts should target informational keywords that support the service page without competing with it.
