## Website Platform Assessment

### Current Platform: Wix

**Issue:** The website is built on Wix, which we do not recommend for businesses serious about long-term SEO growth and digital ownership.

**Key concerns with Wix:**

- **You never own the code or the website.** If you ever leave Wix, you cannot take your site with you. You lose your templates, your page structures, and your design work. You are renting, not owning.
- **Limited SEO control.** Wix restricts access to server-level configurations, advanced URL structures, and technical SEO elements that other platforms provide (e.g., WordPress, Webflow).
- **Auto-generated URL patterns.** The duplicate pages on this site (`/about-8`, `/about-9-5`, `/about-8-3`, `/copy-of-dental-implant-surgery`) are a direct result of how Wix handles page creation and duplication. These messy URLs hurt SEO and user experience.
- **Performance limitations.** Wix sites often load slower than custom-built or WordPress sites due to platform overhead, bloated code output, and limited caching control.
- **Vendor lock-in.** Migrating away from Wix requires rebuilding the entire site from scratch since you cannot export your site's code, design, or CMS structure.

**Recommendation:** If the website is to be rebuilt or significantly overhauled, we strongly recommend migrating to a platform where you own your code and website outright, such as WordPress or Webflow. This is a long-term investment in digital asset ownership and SEO flexibility.

## 1. Backlink Profile Analysis

### Current State

| Metric | Value | Assessment |
|--------|-------|------------|
| Total Backlinks | ~1,400 | Inflated by spam |
| Domain Authority | Low | Poor authority signals |
| Spammy/Toxic Links | Majority of 1.4k total | Critical issue |
| Quality Referring Domains | Very few | Needs building |

### Issues Identified

**Issue: Toxic Backlink Profile**
- **Impact:** HIGH
- **Evidence:** The site has accumulated approximately 1,400 backlinks, but the vast majority are spammy or toxic in nature. This artificially inflated link count does not translate into authority. In fact, a high volume of spammy backlinks can trigger Google penalties or algorithmic suppression, actively hurting rankings.
- **Why it matters:** Google evaluates backlink quality, not just quantity. A dental practice with 1,400 links from spam directories, link farms, or irrelevant foreign-language sites sends negative trust signals. The low domain authority despite the link count confirms that Google is not crediting these links.

### Recommendations

1. **Conduct a full backlink audit** using Semrush, Ahrefs, or Google Search Console's Links report. Export all backlinks and categorize them as healthy, suspicious, or toxic.
2. **Create a disavow file** for Google Search Console listing all toxic/spammy domains. Submit via the Google Disavow Tool to tell Google to ignore these links.
3. **Manually request link removal** from the most harmful linking domains where possible (contact webmasters).
4. **Build legitimate local backlinks** through:
   - Local Chamber of Commerce membership
   - Dental association directories (ADA, state dental associations)
   - Sponsoring local community events, school programs, or sports teams
   - Guest posts or interviews on local health/wellness blogs
   - Partnerships with complementary local businesses (orthodontists, pediatricians, pharmacies)
5. **Monitor backlinks monthly** to catch new spam links early and disavow promptly.

## 3. Duplicate & Unnecessary Pages

### Issues Identified

- **Evidence:** The following pages are duplicates, copies, or orphaned content that confuse search engines and waste crawl budget:

| Problem URL | Issue | Action |
|-------------|-------|--------|

| `/about-9-5` | Duplicate About page (Wix auto-generated) | 301 redirect to `/about` |

| `/read-more` | Vague, likely orphaned page | 301 redirect to `/about` or remove |

- **Why it matters:** These duplicate pages cause several problems:
  - **Keyword cannibalization:** Multiple pages competing for the same keywords dilute ranking signals.
  - **Crawl budget waste:** Google spends time crawling duplicate pages instead of valuable content.
  - **Link equity dilution:** Any backlinks pointing to duplicate pages split authority instead of consolidating it.
  - **Poor user experience:** Visitors may land on outdated or incomplete duplicate pages.
  - **Wix platform artifact:** These URL patterns (`/about-8`, `/copy-of-`) are a direct result of Wix's page management system, which auto-generates these slugs when pages are duplicated or recreated.

2. **Audit all 28 pages** for additional duplicates or thin/orphaned content not listed here.
3. **Consolidate the About section** into a single, comprehensive About page with doctor bios, practice history, mission, and credentials.
4. **Ensure canonical tags** are properly set on all remaining pages to prevent any further duplicate content signals.
5. **After redirects are in place**, request re-crawling of the affected URLs through Google Search Console.

## 4. Local SEO Assessment

### Issues Identified

**Issue: Missing Local Listings and Local SEO Opportunities**
- **Impact:** HIGH
- **Evidence:** The practice is missing key local business listings and has not fully optimized for local search visibility. For a dental practice, local SEO is the primary driver of new patient acquisition, as most patients search for "dentist near me" or "dentist in [city]."

### Local SEO Checklist

| Element | Status | Priority |
|---------|--------|----------|
| Google Business Profile (fully optimized) | Needs verification | Critical |
| Consistent NAP across all listings | Needs audit | Critical |
| Apple Maps / Apple Business Connect | Likely missing | High |
| Bing Places for Business | Likely missing | High |
| Yelp Business Page | Needs verification | High |
| Healthgrades Profile | Needs verification | High |
| Zocdoc Listing | Likely missing | Medium |
| WebMD / Vitals.com | Likely missing | Medium |
| 1-800-Dentist | Likely missing | Medium |
| Local Chamber of Commerce | Likely missing | Medium |
| BBB (Better Business Bureau) | Likely missing | Medium |
| Facebook Business Page | Needs verification | Medium |
| Instagram Business Profile | Needs verification | Medium |
| Nextdoor Business Page | Likely missing | Medium |

### Recommendations

1. **Google Business Profile (GBP) Optimization** - This is the single highest-impact local SEO action:
   - Ensure the profile is claimed and fully verified
   - Complete every field: hours, services, insurance accepted, payment methods, accessibility features
   - Add high-quality real photos of the office, staff, and treatment rooms (not stock photos)
   - Post weekly GBP updates (offers, tips, news)
   - Respond to every review (positive and negative) within 24-48 hours
   - Add a Q&A section with commonly asked questions
   - Use the Services and Products editor to list all treatments offered
   - Enable messaging and appointment booking

2. **NAP Consistency (Name, Address, Phone):**
   - Audit every listing to ensure the business name, address, and phone number are identical everywhere
   - Even small variations ("Suite 100" vs "Ste. 100" vs "#100") can confuse Google
   - Use a service like BrightLocal or Moz Local to identify and fix inconsistencies

3. **Build out all missing directory listings** as identified in the checklist above.

4. **Generate and manage patient reviews:**
   - Create a simple post-visit review request process (email or text with direct Google review link)
   - Aim for a steady stream of 4-5 new reviews per month
   - Respond to all reviews professionally

5. **Add LocalBusiness schema markup** to the website (see Section 6).

6. **Create location-specific content** targeting "[service] in [city/neighborhood]" keywords.

## 5. Design, Imagery & Trust Signals

### Issues Identified

**Issue: Over-Reliance on Stock Photography**
- **Impact:** MEDIUM
- **Evidence:** The website uses too many stock images throughout. For a dental practice, stock photos of smiling models, generic dental equipment, and staged office scenes erode patient trust and make the practice feel impersonal.
- **Why it matters for SEO:** Google's E-E-A-T framework (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) applies heavily to health-related websites (YMYL - Your Money or Your Life). Real photos of the actual doctors, staff, office, and patients (with consent) are trust signals that impact both conversions and search quality assessment.

**Issue: Overall Design Needs Revision**
- **Impact:** MEDIUM
- **Evidence:** The current design does not meet modern standards for a healthcare practice website. Poor design increases bounce rates, reduces time on site, and hurts conversion rates, all of which are indirect SEO ranking signals.

### Recommendations

1. **Replace stock photos with real photography:**
   - Professional headshots of all doctors and staff
   - Office interior and exterior photos
   - Treatment room photos
   - Before/after photos (with patient consent and proper HIPAA compliance)
   - Team photos and community involvement photos
   - Equipment and technology photos

2. **Design improvements to prioritize:**
   - Clear, prominent call-to-action buttons ("Book Appointment," "Call Now")
   - Mobile-responsive design that loads fast on phones
   - Easy-to-find contact information on every page (click-to-call on mobile)
   - Professional color scheme consistent with the brand
   - Clean, modern layout with sufficient white space
   - Patient testimonials prominently displayed
   - Trust badges (ADA membership, insurance logos, awards)

3. **Improve page load speed:**
   - Compress all images (use WebP format)
   - Minimize unnecessary scripts and plugins
   - Ensure Wix site performance settings are optimized (or consider platform migration)

| Page Speed | Likely needs improvement | Test with PageSpeed Insights, optimize images |
| URL Structure | Poor (Wix defaults) | Clean up where possible, redirect duplicates |

### Schema Markup (Structured Data)

The following schema types should be implemented on the website:

- **LocalBusiness (Dentist)** - on every page (or at minimum the homepage and contact page):
  - Business name, address, phone, hours
  - Geo coordinates
  - Services offered
  - Insurance accepted
  - Price ranges

- **FAQPage** - on service pages with FAQ sections

- **BreadcrumbList** - for site navigation signals

- **Review/AggregateRating** - if patient reviews are displayed on the site

- **MedicalBusiness** - additional healthcare-specific structured data

> **Note:** Schema detection cannot be performed via automated fetch on Wix sites, as Wix injects JSON-LD via client-side JavaScript. Verify existing schema using Google's Rich Results Test (https://search.google.com/test/rich-results).

### Internal Linking

With only 28 pages, the internal linking structure is naturally limited. As content is expanded:
- Link service pages to related blog posts
- Link blog posts back to relevant service pages
- Create a clear hierarchy: Homepage > Service Category > Individual Service
- Add breadcrumb navigation
- Include contextual links within body content (not just navigation menus)
