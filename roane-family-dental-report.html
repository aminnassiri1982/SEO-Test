Technical SEO Issues

CRITICAL: Website returns 403 errors to crawlers and bots. robots.txt and sitemap.xml are inaccessible. If Googlebot faces the same blocks, the site is being actively de-indexed. Check server/hosting security settings immediately.
CRITICAL: Address inconsistency across the web — Yelp and older listings show "1673 10th St" while newer listings show "2180 8th Ct #100". Google penalizes NAP (Name, Address, Phone) inconsistency in local search rankings.

⚠ Blog Strategy & Keyword Cannibalization Risk

Roane Family Dental currently has zero blog content. When launching a blog, it is critical to avoid keyword cannibalization — where blog posts compete with service pages for the same search terms, confusing Google and hurting both pages' rankings.

Key Rule: Service pages target transactional keywords (e.g., "dental implants west linn"). Blog posts target informational keywords that support the service page without competing with it.

Cannibalization Examples to Avoid

Example 1: Dental Implants
✅ Service Page (Exists)
"Dental Implants West Linn, OR"
Target: "dental implants west linn"
vs.
❌ Blog Post (Avoid This)
"Dental Implants in West Linn — Everything You Need to Know"
Target: "dental implants west linn"
💡 Fix: Instead, write "How Long Do Dental Implants Last?" or "Dental Implants vs. Dentures: Which Is Right for You?" — these answer informational queries and link back to the Implants service page.
Example 2: Family Dentistry
✅ Service Page (Exists)
"Family Dentistry West Linn, OR"
Target: "family dentist west linn"
vs.
❌ Blog Post (Avoid This)
"Best Family Dentist in West Linn"
Target: "family dentist west linn"
💡 Fix: Instead, write "At What Age Should Your Child First Visit the Dentist?" or "How to Make Dental Visits Fun for Kids" — these attract parents searching for info and funnel them to the Family Dentistry page.
Example 3: Periodontal Care
✅ Service Page (Exists)
"Periodontal Care West Linn, OR"
Target: "gum disease treatment west linn"
vs.
❌ Blog Post (Avoid This)
"Periodontal Care in West Linn, OR"
Target: "periodontal care west linn"
💡 Fix: Instead, write "5 Warning Signs of Gum Disease You Shouldn't Ignore" or "Can Gum Disease Cause Heart Problems?" — informational intent that links to the Periodontal Care page.
