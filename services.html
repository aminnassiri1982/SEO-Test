What We Offer

Services

Practical leadership development and coaching for dental practices that want to lead better, operate better, and grow more intentionally.

Service Programs
01

Leadership Study Club Seminar Series

Designed for practice owners, office managers, and key leaders who want practical leadership development in a collaborative setting.

Topics may include leadership, communication, accountability, office manager development, systems, meeting rhythm, and growth-focused execution.

Leadership Communication Accountability Office Manager Development Meeting Rhythm
02

Coaching for Existing Practices

Customized coaching for established practices ready to strengthen leadership, communication, accountability, systems, and growth.

This work may begin with a complimentary discovery conversation and, when appropriate, an in-person discovery phase to assess leadership structure, team dynamics, operational opportunities, and growth barriers.

Leadership Structure Team Dynamics Systems Growth Barriers
03

Coaching for Practice Owners Buying or Building a New Office

Designed for doctors entering a startup, acquisition, or transition phase who want to create stronger leadership structure from the beginning.

This work helps establish role clarity, communication standards, accountability, operational rhythm, and a healthier foundation for growth.

Role Clarity Communication Standards Operational Rhythm Growth Foundation
04

6- to 12-Month Leadership Development Program

A structured leadership development engagement for practices that want deeper transformation, more consistent implementation, and sustained support over time.

Available in either a 6-month or 12-month format, depending on the needs of the practice.

Deep Transformation Consistent Implementation Sustained Support 6 or 12 Months
Across All Programs
