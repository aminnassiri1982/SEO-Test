## 8. Opportunities & Recommendations

### High Priority

#### 1. Fix Crawler Access Issues
- **Action:** Verify that Googlebot, Bingbot, and social media crawlers are not blocked by the server configuration (WAF, Cloudflare, etc.)
- **Action:** Ensure `/robots.txt` and `/sitemap.xml` are accessible to search engine bots
- **Impact:** Critical - if crawlers are blocked, the site may lose indexation over time

#### 2. Target "Dentist San Juan Capistrano" Keyword
- **Action:** Create or optimize a dedicated page targeting "dentist in San Juan Capistrano, CA" as the primary keyword
- **Action:** Ensure the homepage or main location page has strong content, schema markup, and internal links targeting this keyword
- **Impact:** High - this is the most searched general dental keyword for their area and they're missing from the top 10

#### 3. Optimize Homepage Title Tag
- **Action:** Shorten from "Ortega Dental Care | Dental implants in San Juan Capistrano | 27231 Ortega Hwy. #A, San Juan Capistrano, CA 92675" to something like "Ortega Dental Care | Dentist in San Juan Capistrano, CA | Implants & General Dentistry"
- **Impact:** High - current title is too long and will be truncated in search results, reducing CTR

### Medium Priority

#### 4. Create Nearby City Landing Pages
- **Action:** Build dedicated landing pages for each service area city:
  - `/mission-viejo-ca/dentist/`
  - `/laguna-niguel-ca/dentist/`
  - `/san-clemente-ca/dentist/`
  - `/dana-point-ca/dentist/`
  - `/ladera-ranch-ca/dentist/`
- **Impact:** Medium-High - capture search traffic from surrounding cities where potential patients are searching

#### 5. Consolidate Duplicate-Intent Pages
- **Action:** Evaluate and potentially merge or redirect `/san-juan-capistrano-ca/dental-center/`, `/san-juan-capistrano-ca/dental-practice/`, and `/san-juan-capistrano-ca/dental-office/` - these target nearly identical search intent
- **Impact:** Medium - reduces cannibalization and consolidates link equity

#### 6. Implement/Verify Schema Markup
- **Action:** Add or verify LocalBusiness, Dentist, and MedicalBusiness schema markup on key pages
- **Action:** Add FAQ schema to service pages
- **Action:** Add Review/AggregateRating schema if reviews are displayed
- **Impact:** Medium - improves rich snippet eligibility and SERP visibility

### Lower Priority

#### 7. Increase Review Volume
- **Action:** Implement a systematic review generation strategy (follow-up emails/texts after appointments)
- **Action:** Focus on Google Business Profile reviews specifically
- **Impact:** Medium - 90 Yelp reviews over 20 years is low; competitors may have more

#### 8. Content Marketing / Blog Strategy
- **Action:** Publish regular blog content targeting informational queries:
  - "How much do dental implants cost in Orange County?"
  - "All-on-4 vs traditional dentures"
  - "What to expect during a dental implant procedure"
  - "Best orthodontic options for adults in San Juan Capistrano"
- **Impact:** Medium - builds topical authority and captures top-of-funnel search traffic

#### 9. Social Media Optimization
- **Action:** Increase posting frequency on Facebook (1,258 likes is modest for 20+ years)
- **Action:** Grow Instagram presence with before/after photos, patient testimonials, educational content
- **Impact:** Low-Medium - social signals support local SEO and brand awareness

#### 10. Clean Up URL Structure
- **Action:** Rename `/about-5` to `/patient-portal`
- **Action:** Ensure all URLs follow a consistent, descriptive pattern
- **Impact:** Low - minor UX and crawlability improvement
